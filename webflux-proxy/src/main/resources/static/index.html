Cookies

Both the backend (/path1/api/cookies) and the proxy's own "main app" (/main/cookies) set a JSESSIONID. Through a proxy, the backend's cookies are stored as PX_* under Path=/path1, and only those are forwarded to it (prefix removed). The /main endpoints exist only on the proxies.
